About Us

Your trusted partner in agricultural products

Who we are

Built for Malawian farmers

Eagle Agribusiness & Extension Services is a Malawian agricultural inputs and extension company founded and led by Mr Titus Chiwindo, CEO & Founder. We are a trusted partner to farmers across Lilongwe, Blantyre and Mzuzu, supplying quality manure, fertiliser, and ready-to-transplant seedlings — while promoting improved productivity, food security, and better livelihoods for farming communities.

Operating under the wider Eagle Investment brand, we run a growing network of official selling points across the Central and Southern regions, making agricultural inputs accessible and affordable to smallholder and commercial farmers alike. Our Facebook community has grown to over 21,000 followers — farmers who trust us for honest advice and reliable products.

Why we do this
